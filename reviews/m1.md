## Issues

### m1-p07 - major - variation ambiguous about the fixed lead
**What was wrong:** The variation said "Nobody is on leave, but the team must include at least one of two particular senior analysts. How many teams of four from the 10?" It removed the leave constraint but was silent on whether the lead analyst is still forced onto the team. The variationAnswer, C(10,4) - C(8,4) = 140, assumes the lead constraint is also dropped; a student keeping the lead would compute C(9,3) - C(7,3) = 84 - 35 = 49 and be marked wrong for a defensible reading.
**Fix:** Reworded to "Drop both constraints: nobody is on leave and no one is forced onto the team. Instead, the team of four must include at least one of two particular senior analysts. How many teams can be chosen from the 10?" The answer 140 is now the only reading.

### m1-p02 - minor - fastPath was confusing
**What was wrong:** The fastPath read "...times 0.833 is 0.579, squared again... faster: 0.694^2 = 0.482...". The abandoned "squared again" branch would compute (5/6)^6, and the half-finished sentence is not a fast path.
**Fix:** Rewritten as a clean square-square-multiply: (5/6)^2 ~ 0.694, (5/6)^4 ~ 0.482, (5/6)^5 ~ 0.402, answer ~ 0.60.

### m1-p04 - minor - hint 2 said "the last position"
**What was wrong:** "The C takes the last position" can be read as position 7 of the string, which would wrongly suggest the cancel is always last (that is the variation, not the problem).
**Fix:** "The C takes the one position left over."

### m1-p06 - minor - "the chair" in a problem about seats
**What was wrong:** The solution said "sit the chair anywhere; her seat becomes the reference point" and the variation said "The chair and the deputy". The committee chair is never introduced in the prompt, and in a seating problem "chair" reads first as a seat.
**Fix:** "sit the committee chair (or any one member) in any seat; that seat becomes the reference point ... because the committee chair is pinned", and "The committee chair and the deputy insist on...".

### m1-p11 - minor - variation did not say what happened to the two-volume handbook
**What was wrong:** "Three volumes of a three-volume set must stay together, among six books in total" left open whether the original two-volume pair is still constrained. The answer 4! x 3! = 144 assumes only the three-volume block is restricted.
**Fix:** "Instead, three of the six books are the volumes of a three-volume set and must stay together (in any internal order); the other three books have no restriction."

### m1-p19 - minor - hint 1 referred to player numbers that the prompt never assigns
**What was wrong:** "Take the lowest-numbered player who is not yet matched" presupposes a numbering introduced only in the solution.
**Fix:** "Number the players 1 to 10 and take the lowest-numbered player who is not yet matched."

### m1-p24 - minor - tangled sentence in the subtraction route
**What was wrong:** "Unrestricted divisions into three unlabelled teams of four: 12!/(4!)^3 = 34650 ordered divisions, divided by 3! for the team order, gives 5775" mixes "unlabelled" and "ordered" in one clause.
**Fix:** "With no restriction, there are 12!/(4!)^3 = 34650 ways to fill three *labelled* teams; dividing by 3! for the team order gives 5775 divisions into unlabelled teams."
