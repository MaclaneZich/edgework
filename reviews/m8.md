## Critical

### m8-p12 — solution: wrong statement about the quote that shuts out the peeker
- **Wrong:** "you would need a quote at least 5.5 at 8.5 before they could no longer profit on any face". At 5.5/8.5 the counterparty still buys on a 6 (fair 9.5, edge 1) and sells on a 1 (fair 4.5, edge 1); the problem's own variationAnswer computes that profit as 1/3. Only 4.5 at 9.5 (or wider) removes every profitable face.
- **Fix:** rewritten to "Even widening to 5.5 at 8.5 leaves them an edge of 1 on faces 1 and 6; only a quote of 4.5 at 9.5 (or wider) shuts them out on every face".

### m8-p22 — commonMistake: wrong claim about where the factor-4 error lands
- **Wrong:** "a factor of 4 error, which here still lands on 10^12 only by luck". 4 × 1.84e12 = 7.37e12; log10 = 12.87, rounds to 10^13 (and in plain distance it is 6.4e12 from 10^12 vs 2.6e12 from 10^13). The error changes the answer.
- **Fix:** commonMistake now says the slip gives about 7.4e12, which rounds to 10^13 and gets the question wrong.

## Major

### m8-p11 — commonMistake describes the correct computation as the mistake
- **Wrong:** "treating all trades as earning 4 then subtracting 10 for informed" is 0.8·4 + 0.2·(4 − 10) = 2, which is exactly the correct h − qD route. A student reading it would be told their right method is wrong.
- **Fix:** the mistake is now charging the informed trade the full edge 10 instead of 10 − 4 = 6, which gives 3.2 − 2 = 1.2.

### m8-p24 — prompt ambiguous on compounding
- **Wrong:** "Cash earns simple interest of 5% per period: one dollar becomes 1.05 dollars after one period." Under simple interest over two periods a dollar becomes 1.10, but the solution (and verifier) discount by 1.05² = 1.1025. A strong student could legitimately read 1.10.
- **Fix:** prompt now reads "Cash grows by 5% per period, compounded each period: one dollar becomes 1.05 dollars after one period and 1.1025 dollars after two." Answer unchanged.
