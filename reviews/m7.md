## Critical

- **m7-p21 solution and commonMistake** - wrong side remark. The solution said Ben "can afford to fold 40% of the time rather than the 33% a naive 'bet over pot-plus-bet' rule would suggest". With pot 4 and bet 1 the naive rule gives bet/(pot + bet) = 1/5 = 20% folds (equivalently c = 4/5, the answer you get from -3c + 2(1 - c) = -2 when you forget that half the bluffs run into High); 33% is the pot-2 number and belongs to a different game. Rewritten with 20% and the reason. The commonMistake said the omission "changes the equation from 1 to 3 odds to the 3/5 answer", which does not describe any computation; it now states the wrong equation and the wrong answer it produces (c = 4/5).
