## Issues

### lesson card 1 ("Structure a search"), example line — critical (wrong statement), fixed
- Wrong: "2^4 = 16 states, two of them forbidden". A forbidden state is acid and base in the same room with the technician elsewhere; the buffer can be in either room, so there are 4 forbidden states, not 2 (confirmed by enumeration).
- Fix: "four of them forbidden (acid and base together away from the technician, buffer anywhere)".

### m9-p04 commonMistake — major (misleading mistake note), fixed
- Wrong: "Answering 20 or 11 by instinct ... or using 21 holes (the possible sums)". 11 is the correct answer, so the note tells the student their right answer is a mistake; "21 holes" does not correspond to anything in the problem (there are 37 possible sums and 20 badge numbers).
- Fix: "Answering 10 (the number of pairs) or 20 (all the badges) by instinct without building the worst case, or taking the holes to be the 20 badge numbers instead of the 10 complementary pairs."

### m9-p11 commonMistake — major (false claim), fixed
- Wrong: "Using only the parity of the total ... cannot separate (B) from (C) correctly". Totals are (A) 9, (B) 8, (C) 17; the parity of the total rejects both (A) and (C) and admits (B), so for this problem it gives the right classification. The note as written contradicts the problem's own numbers.
- Fix: the note now says total parity happens to work here but is too weak in general, with a concrete counterexample (corners 2, edges 1, centre 0: total 12, dark minus light 4, unreachable), and keeps the "invariant does not prove reachability" warning.

### m9-p23 solution, optimality paragraph — minor (loose argument), tightened
- The sentence "sends c and d together (which forces the pairing pattern ...)" was not quite right: c and d can also cross together first, which is not the pairing pattern (it costs at least d + c + b + 2a, no better than the escort). Rewrote the paragraph to cover the three cases (together first, together second, separately) so the min(2a+b+c+d, a+3b+d) claim follows. Verified against Dijkstra for both the problem and the variation.

### m9-p20 variationAnswer — minor (wording), fixed
- "the first analyst alone is right only half the time" refers to a "first" analyst in a simultaneous game. Changed to "any single analyst is right only half the time, and a win needs that analyst to be right."

### m9-p06 fastPath — minor (wording), fixed
- "from 1, 1, 1; thirteen entries later you read 60" miscounts (60 is the thirteenth entry, a_12). Changed to "the thirteenth entry, a_12, is 60".

### lesson outro — minor (wording), fixed
- "which of the eight cards in this lesson" but the lesson has nine cards. Changed to "which of the technique cards in this lesson".
